Why we made it

A drawing of a system can be wrong for years and nobody notices. So we stopped drawing.

Explainer writes a tiny copy of the real thing. MapReduce really counts the words. Raft really elects a leader. Every number you see came out of that run, and you can rewind it, change it and break it.

Then it teaches the way a patient teacher would. You meet the problem before the fix and one idea at a time, and just before the interesting part it stops to ask what you think happens next.
